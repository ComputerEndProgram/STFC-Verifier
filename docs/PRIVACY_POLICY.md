# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the STFC Verifier bot (the "Bot") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use it.

## 1. Who We Are

The Bot is developed and operated by:

> **ComputerEndProgram**  
> Support server: <https://discord.gg/qnmzeNAea3>

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us through the support server linked above.

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use the Bot, the following data may be collected and stored:

### Verification Data

- **Discord user ID** — stored when you begin or complete the verification process.
- **Discord guild (server) ID** — stored to associate your verification with the specific server you verified in.
- **Player link and player ID** — the stfc.pro link and in-game player ID you submit during verification.
- **Player data** — information fetched from stfc.pro at the time of verification, including your in-game username, level, server number, alliance tag, and rank.
- **Verification status and timestamp** — records of when you were verified.

### Logs

The Bot keeps records of verification actions (e.g., user verified, recalled) including the affected user ID, the action taken, the acting administrator's user ID when applicable, and a timestamp.

### Web Session Data (Admin Interface)

If you use the web-based admin interface, the Bot may process Discord OAuth data to authenticate you and identify which servers you administer. This is used solely to authorize access to server configuration.

### Wizard Sessions

While a verification is in progress, the Bot temporarily stores a session for your user ID, including any screenshot you submit for manual review. Sessions expire automatically and are deleted after the verification is completed or expires.

## 3. How We Use the Data

We use the collected data solely to:

- Operate the verification process and assign the appropriate roles in your Discord server.
- Display your verified player information to server administrators.
- Run periodic checks to keep player information up to date.
- Maintain verification logs for moderation and administrative purposes.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

## 4. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored in a local SQLite database operated by ComputerEndProgram on the hosting infrastructure used for the Bot. No data is transmitted to third parties except as needed to fetch public player information from stfc.pro during verification.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Verification data** is retained for as long as the corresponding server uses the Bot, or until you (or a server administrator) request removal of your verification.
- **Verification logs** are retained for administrative purposes.
- **Wizard sessions** are deleted automatically after the verification flow is completed or the session expires.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us through the support server: <https://discord.gg/qnmzeNAea3>.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated version will be made available in this document. Your continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 9. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the support server: <https://discord.gg/qnmzeNAea3>.
